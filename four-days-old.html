We are at the very beginning

Stars will keep forming and burning for roughly 100 trillion years. The universe is 13.8 billion years into that. Squeeze the whole age of stars into an 80-year human life, and today the universe is about four days old.

Directional joy

Every good thing is a fall done well

A surfer doesn't fight the wave. They let it fall and shape the fall. That is what everything alive does. Sunlight falls from the Sun's 5,800 kelvin surface down to the cold of space, and on the way down it gets caught by leaves, turned into sugar, and turned into you. Prigogine called these shapes dissipative structures: hurricanes, cells, minds. Order that exists because it rides the slope well.

So the joy you feel skiing or surfing isn't a metaphor laid on top of physics. It's the physics, felt from the inside.

1 ⁄ 9,000 of the slope

That is how much of the sunlight reaching Earth all of human civilization uses today. Everything we have ever built, every city and every song, runs on a sliver of the wave we are standing on.

Wholes becoming parts of larger wholes

The ladder has no top rung

Maynard Smith and Szathmáry traced life's great leaps. Each time, things that had lived alone joined into a new whole. Every rung so far was built by blind selection, and usually the parts gave up their freedom to the whole.

  1. Molecules into replicatorsChemistry learns to copy itself.
  2. Genes into chromosomesCopiers bind together and share a fate.
  3. Simple cells into complex cellsOne cell swallows another, and both live on as one.
  4. Cells into bodies37 trillion cells, one point of view: you.
  5. Bodies into societiesAnts, bees, and people who share a language.
  6. Minds into something we chooseSpeculation, offered as a possibility: the first transition built on purpose, where the parts keep their freedom and gain each other. Connection that adds to each mind instead of absorbing it.

David Deutsch's claim is that anything the laws of physics allow can be done, given the right knowledge. A world with less and less suffering is not forbidden by any law we know. So the question is not whether it's possible. It's how long the learning takes, and that depends on who keeps learning.

For the next few thousand years

A practice for the long ride

Zoom

Look closer than you need to. Wonder is renewable. Every layer has another under it.

Record

Leave good traces. A memory is a footprint pointing back to its cause. Make yours point at kindness.

Lessen

Take one unit of suffering out of the world when you can. It is the one quantity worth driving to zero.

Ride

When you fall, fall in a direction. Downhill is where all the energy is.

Join

Connect in ways that leave both minds larger. That might be the next rung.
