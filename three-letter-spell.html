The Three-Letter Spell

Everything you have ever touched is written in an alphabet of three letters. Learning to spell in it is the closest thing to real magic.

1 · The alphabet

A rose and a rock are the same letters

Two up quarks and a down make a proton. One up and two downs make a neutron. Add electrons and you have every atom there is. A rose, a rock, your hand and a star differ only in how the three letters are arranged.

2 · The letters are free

What's scarce is the spelling

The universe hands out the letters almost without limit. What's rare is knowing how to arrange them. An oak builds itself mostly out of air: the carbon in wood comes from carbon dioxide. It runs on sunlight and follows a recipe folded inside an acorn.

3 · A machine that builds anything

Von Neumann drew life before we saw it

In 1948 John von Neumann worked out what a machine would need to build anything, including copies of itself. Five years later, the structure of DNA showed that life had been running his design for nearly four billion years.

4 · The leap

Let the idea die instead of you

Evolution tests every new recipe by letting the failures die. A mind can test a recipe by imagining it. Karl Popper called this letting our hypotheses die in our stead. Imagination is the first way of making new things that doesn't need suffering to test them.

David Deutsch and Chiara Marletto's constructor theory recasts physics as a list of which transformations are possible and which are impossible. Anything possible is then a matter of knowledge. In that sense, posthuman means closing the gap between imagining a recipe and running it.

5 · The twist

Magic was always a kind of spelling

spell

In Old English, a spell was a story or a saying. The word for spelling out letters and the word for an incantation grow from the same Germanic root.

glamour

An enchantment. It began as a Scots form of grammar, which in the Middle Ages meant learning of any kind, occult learning included. Walter Scott made it famous.

grammar

The rules for arranging letters so they mean something. For matter, those rules are physics.

Matter is written in three letters. Learning to spell in it is the closest thing to real magic.
